# v3.24.1 — categories from any reconciled entry

- The statement-matches pull also records the booking lines (GL account,
  amount) of every invoice, bill or journal entry a bank line is
  reconciled with, so payments matched to entries that are not cached
  bills or invoices get a category too.
- New category `accrual` ("Previous-year invoices", accounts 404/444): a
  bill that settles an invoice accrued the year before.
- `metadata.documents` lists every matched entry's URI.
