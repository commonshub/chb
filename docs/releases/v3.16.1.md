# v3.16.1 — documents belong to the contact's company

A bill or invoice addressed to a contact person inside a company (Odoo
display name "XL Collective SRL, Leen Schelfhout") is now published under
the company. Before, the contact, who inherits the company's VAT number in
Odoo, was published as a named sole trader. The pull stores the company
(`commercialPartner`) with each document; older caches fall back to the
display name. The contact's name stays in the stewards tier
(`contact.person`). Also recognises the Czech/Slovak legal form "s.r.o.".
