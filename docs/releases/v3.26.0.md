# v3.26.0 — exclude transactions from totals (test mints)

- A trusted annotation with an `["exclude", "<reason>"]` tag leaves a
  record out of every total: it stays in `transactions.json`, marked
  `metadata.excluded`, and `summary.json` (flows, accounts, currencies,
  token report, transaction count, coverage) and contributors' token totals
  skip it. `chb nostr annotate <uri> --exclude "test mint"` publishes one;
  `--exclude ""` includes the record again.
- `settings/excluded-transactions.json` entries with `"keep": true` do the
  same without Nostr (by tx hash). The Celo CHT test mints and their burns
  are listed: 1,000,000 CHT in January 2025 and 2,000,000,000,000 CHT in
  September 2025 (one mint, three burns), which inflated the 2025 token
  totals to two trillion.
- Docs: annotations.md (the `exclude` tag), website.md §13.
