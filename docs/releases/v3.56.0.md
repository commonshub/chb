# v3.56.0

- Member recaps now show what the member paid the hub over the period (`contribution=`), and what their organisation paid (`orgName=`, `orgContribution=`). Amounts include VAT, as received.
  - **Personal:** Stripe charges whose payer email hashes to the member's emailHash, minus refunds, plus payments received on Odoo customer invoices of the member's own partner (by odooPartnerId, or a partner whose email hashes to theirs), minus credit notes. Invoice payments booked on a Stripe journal are left out, since the Stripe charge already counts them.
  - **Organisation:** the company the member's Odoo contact belongs to; without one, the Discord bot's invoice-orgs.json (`settings/invoice-orgs.json` or `CHB_INVOICE_ORGS_FILE`), when it names exactly one partner. Never guessed from names. Its amount is the payments received on all its customer invoices in the period.
- The preview shows both amounts per member. `--send-test` accepts `--email-hash` next to `--discord`, to fill in the amounts for the Discord version.
