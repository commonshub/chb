# v3.24.0 — categories from Odoo, a published taxonomy, coverage

- **Categories from Odoo.** A transaction that rules leave uncategorised
  takes its category from how its bank line is booked in Odoo:
  - reconciled with invoices or bills: their lines' category, with the
    documents' URIs in `metadata.documents`;
  - booked straight to an account: that account's category (580000 →
    `internal_transfer` with type `INTERNAL`, 451 → `vat`, 455/62 → `HR`, …).

  Marked `metadata.categorySource: "odoo"`, and never pushed back to Odoo.
- `chb odoo pull` also archives which invoices and bills each reconciled
  bank line settles (`latest/providers/odoo/<db>/statement-matches.json`,
  read-only). `chb generate` rebuilds a month when its bank lines are
  booked or reconciled differently in Odoo, including months outside the
  recent window.
- **Opening balances.** The "Solde d'ouverture" row a bank journal starts
  with is category `opening_balance`, type `INTERNAL`: no longer income.
- Rule: Monerium memos starting with "Internal transfer" are internal
  transfers.
- **Taxonomy.** `settings/categories.json` lists every slug in use with
  `group` and the PCMN account prefixes that map to it (direction can be
  `both`); published as `latest/<tier>/categories.json`.
- **Coverage.** `summary.json` gets `coverage`: euro in/out, the
  uncategorised part, `uncategorisedShare`, and the amount categorised
  from Odoo.
- Docs: [categories.md](../categories.md), website.md §13.
