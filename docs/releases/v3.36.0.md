# v3.36.0 — membership is never confused with rental

Xavier: memberships have no VAT, rentals 21%. A membership is €10/month
(product 94), €100/year (111) or €200/year for an organisation (104), on
704200, MEM journal.

- Invoice lines are `membership` only for those products or a
  membership-named product, without VAT. With VAT they are other income
  (`sales_services` in customers.json). 700000 no longer maps to
  membership (categories.json membership accounts: 7042, 730); lines there
  follow their product.
- Rules `*member*`, `*monthly financial contribution*` and `*MEM/20*` only
  apply to €10/€100/€200, via the new rule matcher `amount_in` (absolute
  gross amounts).
- "Categories check" flags membership payments of other amounts and
  membership-looking invoice lines (membership product/name, 704200) with
  VAT or another price, for the current and previous month.
- Docs: categories.md (Membership is strict), rules.md (`amount_in`).
