# v3.32.1 — only real organisations are named

v3.32.0 named in public every member flagged as an organisation, and the
flag also came from the plan (the yearly non-profit product), so two
individuals on that plan were listed by full name in
`public/members.json` for about ten minutes (cleared by hand). A member
now gets `organizationName` — and appears in public — only when the Odoo
partner is a company or the name carries a legal form (SRL, VZW, ASBL, …).
