# v3.14.0 — vendor bills per audience, and the "still to pay" list

`chb generate` now publishes the Odoo vendor bills in the three tiers:
`YYYY/MM/<tier>/bills.json` (every posted bill and vendor credit note of the
month, paid or not) and `latest/<tier>/pending-bills.json` (every bill still
to pay, whatever its month), so the website can list what the Hub owes and
invite people to help cover it. Public shows business vendors by name with
line descriptions and keeps private individuals anonymous; members also see
individuals; stewards keep contact details, bank account, payments and
attachments. Totals are per currency. `chb bills pending
[--public|--members] [--json]` shows the list.

`chb bills pull` now also refreshes open bills from any month (one light
query, full fetch only for bills whose state moved), so a paid bill leaves
the pending list even when it is older than the pull window.

Documentation: `docs/bills.md`, linked from `docs/website.md` and
`docs/audiences.md`.
