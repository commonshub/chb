# v3.44.4 — Member recaps: checkout links follow the subscription paid

A website `checkout` link now reaches the recap of the member whose
subscription was paid (`paid.subscription`, matched on the members.json id
prefix), not the member whose email was typed at Stripe: someone typing
another member's address can no longer take over that member's recap. A
checkout link without `paid` falls back to the email hash; a subscription
linked to two Discord ids is dropped as ambiguous.
