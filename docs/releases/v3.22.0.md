# v3.22.0 — chart of accounts and ledger balances as open data

- `chb odoo pull` (and so the hourly `chb pull`) also archives, read-only,
  the chart of accounts with its labels in every active language
  (`latest/providers/odoo/<db>/accounts-chart.json`) and, for every year
  from the first posted entry, the balance of every account
  (`YYYY/12/providers/odoo/<db>/ledger-balances.json`: opening, debit,
  credit, closing of posted entries; income and expense openings start at
  the fiscal year from the annual accounts periods).
- `chb generate` publishes them per tier: `latest/<tier>/accounts-chart.json`
  and `YYYY/<tier>/ledger-balances.json` (a year file, present for every
  year).
- Privacy: accounts named after a person (current accounts of directors or
  members, a person's fees) get a neutral label in `public/` and
  `members/` and are merged per two-digit group; payroll (62) is one row;
  account numbers in labels are masked. Settings `accounting.privateAccounts`
  and `accounting.publicAccounts` (account codes) override the detection.
  Totals are the same in every tier.
- Docs: [accounting-data.md](../accounting-data.md#chart-of-accounts-and-ledger-balances),
  website.md §12, audiences.md.
- `docs/agents/accountant.md`: a read-only accountant agent definition for
  Claude Code (copy it to `.claude/agents/` to use it).
