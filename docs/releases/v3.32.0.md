# v3.32.0 — members from Odoo subscriptions: active, grace, lapsed

- The Odoo membership snapshot also keeps churned subscriptions and, per
  subscription, its raw state, end date, partner and invoices (due date,
  payment state, residual).
- `members.json` is built with Odoo subscriptions as the source of truth
  (Stripe for members without one): `status` is `active`, `grace` (unpaid
  past the due date or paused; `graceEndsAt` = 15 days later) or `lapsed`;
  `statusSince`. **Breaking:** `status` no longer carries Stripe's values
  (`past_due`, `canceled`, …); `summary.activeMembers` counts active +
  grace, with new `graceMembers` and `lapsedMembers`.
- Tiers: public lists organisations in good standing by name
  (`organizationName`), never individuals; members lists members in good
  standing only; stewards get everyone, Odoo partner ids and `mismatches`
  between Odoo and Stripe.
- Docs: website.md §16, audiences.md.
