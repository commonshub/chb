# v3.29.0 — bank lines meet their bills before rules book them

- **Vendor reference.** Reconcile also matches a bank line with an open
  bill/invoice whose `ref` or `payment_reference` equals a number in the
  bank text (digits only, leading zeros ignored: KBC "007603556405" ↔
  Proximus ref "7603556405"), same amount ±0.01, unambiguous only.
- **Related partners.** When the line's partner has no open document, the
  documents of every partner holding the same IBAN (by commercial
  partner) are searched: a duplicate contact no longer hides the bills.
- **Guard.** The automatic categorisation (push, categorise, KBC merge) no
  longer books a line to an expense or income account when its party has
  an open document of the same amount; the line stays on 499000 for
  `chb odoo journals <id> reconcile -i`, with a warning. Explicit commands
  (reclassify, review, fix) are unchanged.
- Found by a read-only report: 4 Proximus debits (March–June 2026) booked
  to 616030 while their bills stayed open (fixed in Odoo on 2026-10-05).
