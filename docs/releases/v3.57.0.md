# v3.57.0

- Member recaps are requested with `POST /api/recap` (JSON body). Besides the contributions, the body lists the member's own euro payments over the period as `euroTransactions` (`{at, amount, description}`, from the member's side: a payment is negative, a refund positive; at most 100). The website shows them with the tokens in one Transactions list. Each entry is a Stripe charge or refund matched on the member's email hash, or a payment on an Odoo invoice of their own partner. Descriptions look like "Membership (monthly)", the Stripe product name, "Refund", or "Invoice <number>: <first line>".
- The organisations from the Discord bot are now read live from the website's `GET /api/members/invoice-orgs`. `test-` keys are ignored. The settings/invoice-orgs.json copy is no longer read.
