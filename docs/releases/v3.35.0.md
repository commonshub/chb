# v3.35.0 — every hub payment gets a category

- Invoice lines are categorised by their analytic category, else (customer
  invoices) their product, else their GL account. Income account 700000
  carries rooms, catering and coworking as well as memberships, so a room
  rental reconciled with its invoice used to read as `membership`.
  categories.json gains `products` globs (rental: `*room*`, `*ostrom*`,
  `*event space*`, `*satoshi*`, `down payment*`; coworking: `*cowork*`,
  `*shifter*`; catering: `*coffee*`, `*sandwich*`, `*snack*`, `*buffet*`,
  `*lunch*`, `*catering*`, `drinks*`; ticket: `*ticket*`; membership:
  `*membership*`).
- An incoming payment whose memo is the structured communication of one of
  our posted customer invoices (Monerium mints, KBC transfers) takes that
  invoice's category before Odoo reconciles it, and links the invoice in
  `metadata.documents`.
- A category taken from Odoo now re-stamps `vatAmount` (none for
  membership, donation, rent).
- Rules: small incoming payments (≤ €50) mentioning coffee or drinks →
  `drinks`, when nothing else categorised them.
- `chb generate` warns about commonshub EUR/EURe transactions of the
  current and previous month left without a category.
- Docs: categories.md.
