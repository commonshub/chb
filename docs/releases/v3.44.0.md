# v3.44.0 — Reconcile every sync, by reference first

Every `chb sync` / `chb odoo sync` now reconciles incoming and outgoing
payments with their invoices and bills, and books a line to an account only
when no open document claims it.

## What changed

- **Fresh lines are matched in the sync that creates them.** The live
  reconcile refreshes the journal-lines cache first; before, lines created
  by a push were only refetched after the reconcile (SL 41015 "Factuur 2026
  RCC 72" stayed on 499000).
- **Every push reconciles**, whatever its size (the 20-line cap is gone) and
  even when nothing new arrived, so each sync passes over all unmatched
  lines: on suspense, on an income/expense account, or parked on
  Outstanding Receipts (552002) without being matched to the invoice
  (SL 15392 "MEM/2026/00016").
- **No 30 s timeout around the Odoo push**: an abandoned push kept writing
  (draft → rewrite → repost) while the next step ran. Each Odoo RPC keeps
  its own 120 s HTTP timeout.
- **References**, compared ignoring case and separators ("Factuur 2026 RCC
  72" = 2026_RCC_72, "2025Q2CHI0099"): the document number, sale order
  (invoice origin "S00176"), distinctive vendor and payment references, and
  Belgian structured communications with or without +++ and slashes
  ("000004632152" = +++000/0046/32152+++, mod-97 checked).
- **A memo reference wins over the bank line's partner.** The line is booked
  to the document's partner (Changing World paying De Waele Vincent's
  MEM/2026/00076). The payer's IBAN is no longer registered on a different
  invoice partner.
- **No first-match-wins.** Several open documents of the same partner and
  amount: the one the memo names, otherwise review — never the oldest
  (inFlights 2025Q1CHI0029). The same amount on another partner's invoice
  with no reference is never applied.
- **Foreign-currency bills** (USD Postmark/ActiveCampaign) compare the EUR
  open amount (`amount_residual_signed`) within 3 %: held from being booked
  to an expense account, and offered for review.
- **One matcher for "book it to an account?" and "which invoice?"**: KBC,
  Stripe (new lines, fee lines, the account stage), blockchain/Monerium and
  suspense categorisation all hold a line an open document claims.
- **Closing report**: open invoices/bills whose number, reference or
  structured communication appears on a still-unmatched bank line.

## Upgrade notes

- Journal-line caches are refetched in full once (schema 2 adds the
  counterpart's reconciled flag).
- Invoice/bill caches gain `residualSignedAmount` and `currency`; run
  `chb invoices sync --history` and `chb bills sync --history` once so older
  documents carry them.
