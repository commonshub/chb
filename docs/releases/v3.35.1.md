# v3.35.1 — customers.json income types by product

- customers.json / bookings: `incomeType` per invoice line is the analytic
  tag or product category first (same rule as v3.35.0), else the income
  account. Rooms, catering and coworking booked on 700000 no longer read
  as `membership`, and their customers are no longer flagged as members.
- New incomeType values: `coworking`, `catering`, `drinks`. Existing ones
  unchanged (`room_rental`, `membership`, `tickets_events`, …).
- Room lines on 700000 now also appear in the month's rentals.
- categories.json: fridge products `*fridge*`, `*frige*`.
- Docs: accounting-data.md.
