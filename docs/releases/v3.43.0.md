# v3.43.0 — members.json: salted name-token fingerprints

Every member in the members and stewards tiers of `members.json` gets
`accounts.nameTokens`: one fingerprint per word of their name (Stripe
customer name or Odoo partner name; an organisation's own name), so the
website can match a Discord name to a membership without seeing names.

Each word is normalised (Unicode NFD, accents dropped, lowercase, split on
anything that is not a–z or 0–9, words of two characters or more) and hashed
as `hex(sha256("name:" + word + EMAIL_HASH_SALT))[:16]`; the list is sorted
and distinct. Public `members.json` never carries them.

Test vector: `"dean"` with salt `"salt"` → `80fc3a1415d322ba`.

The fingerprints are computed when members are pulled (`chb members sync`,
part of the hourly `chb pull`), so they appear after the next pull.
