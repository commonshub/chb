# v3.23.0 — closed periods are locked; role accounts are private

- **Lock-date guard.** chb never creates, changes, deletes, posts, resets
  or matches accounting entries dated on or before the lock date: the
  latest of Odoo's company lock dates (`fiscalyear_lock_date`,
  `hard_lock_date`) and the new setting `odoo.lockDate` in
  `settings.json`. It prints the lock date before its first write and
  refuses such calls with a `period locked` error (statement-line imports
  report the line with that reason and carry on). Matching across the lock
  date stays allowed. The check runs in `odooExec`, so it covers every
  command. See operating-principles.md §4.
- **Ledger privacy.** Accounts named after a role one person holds
  ("Current account director", "C/C gérant", président, trésorier,
  zaakvoerder, …) are treated like accounts named after a person: neutral
  label and merged per group in `public/` and `members/`.
- The accountant agent definition (`docs/agents/accountant.md`) states the
  lock-date rule.
