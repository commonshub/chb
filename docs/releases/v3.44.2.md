# v3.44.2 — Reconcile report: no false positives on reversed and split payments

Five of the six lines the v3.44.1 report listed on prod were correct in
Odoo:

- **Reversed documents are closed** (`payment_state = reversed`, cancelled
  by a credit note), like paid ones: never reported as open, and never
  matched by reference — a memo naming a reversed invoice pays its
  replacement, if anything (MEM/2025/00074 → MEM/2026/00055; bill 134).
- **A reference must end where the memo's word ends**: "S00091-1" names the
  sale order S00091-1, not S00091. "Invoice 2026.RCC.47, dated …" still
  names 2026_RCC_47.
- **Partials on the same move are one payment**: a line whose move has a
  receivable/payable line matched, fully or partly, is matched — DelivCo's
  bills paid in two partials from the same move (with a suspense remainder)
  are no longer "paid by another payment", and such a line is never offered
  for another document. Journal-line caches are refetched once (schema 3,
  `documentMatched`).
- Documents `in_payment` (a registered payment waiting for its bank line)
  are not reported: reconcile settles them.
