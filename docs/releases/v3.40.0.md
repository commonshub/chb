# v3.40.0 — room bookings: late bookings and recurring series

Today's "Ecofirst Meeting" (Satoshi, booked 22 Sep) was missing from
bookings.json, with every other October booking made after 21 Sep.

- **Stale month archives.** The calendar sync wrote a month's room-calendar
  archive (YYYY/MM/providers/ics/<room>.ics) once, the first time the
  month had a booking, and never again. From the previous month on, the
  archive is now rewritten whenever the feed differs (including a month
  whose bookings were all cancelled); older months stay frozen unless
  `--force`.
- **Recurring events.** RRULE was ignored, so a weekly series showed only
  its first date. ical now expands DAILY/WEEKLY/MONTHLY/YEARLY rules
  (INTERVAL, COUNT, UNTIL, BYDAY, BYMONTHDAY), EXDATE, moved instances
  (RECURRENCE-ID) and cancelled ones, keeping the local wall clock across
  DST. The sync files a series in every month it recurs in; bookings.json,
  stats and calendar counts expand each month file.
