# v3.34.0 — public #contributions text and 💝praise feed

Xavier decided the content of #contributions and 💝praise is public.

- `contributions.json` gains, per message: `content` (text with `<@id>` →
  `@DisplayName`, `<@&id>` → `@role`, `<#id>` → `#channel`, `<:name:id>` →
  `:name:`, `<t:…>` → Brussels date; third-party IBANs masked, personal
  emails masked at write time), `reactions: [{emoji, count, imageUrl?}]`,
  and `avatarUrl` on author and mentions (users with a custom avatar). No
  field renamed or removed.
- New `praise.json` (💝praise, settings `activities.tokens`), same shape,
  in every month and `latest/` (last 60 days), all tiers. Bots and
  messages without text are dropped. Photos are the public copies already
  in `public/images/`.
- Docs: website.md §14, audiences.md.
