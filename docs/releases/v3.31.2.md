# v3.31.2 — members in past months

A Stripe subscription that is still active counted in a month only when
its current billing period overlapped that month — true for the current
month only. Rebuilding past months' snapshots (`chb members pull
--backfill`) therefore dropped every monthly subscriber from them. An
active subscription now counts in every month from its creation;
cancelled ones until the month they ended, as before.
