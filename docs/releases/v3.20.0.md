# v3.20.0 — annual accounts

New manual source: the association's annual accounts as filed with the
National Bank (abbreviated schema for associations).

- `chb annual-accounts import <files|dirs> [--period START:END] [--filed
  DATE] [--nbb-ref] [--nbb-url] [--label] [--internal FILE] [--dry-run]`
  archives one fiscal year's documents unchanged under
  `YYYY/MM/providers/annual-accounts/<period end>/` with a `filing.json`;
  `chb annual-accounts set <year> --filed DATE | --draft …` changes its
  status; `chb annual-accounts` lists fiscal years. Documents dropped in
  `latest/providers/annual-accounts/` are imported as a draft by `chb pull`.
- Figures are read by NBB code from the abbreviated balance sheet and
  profit and loss PDFs (new dependency: github.com/ledongthuc/pdf), or from
  a `figures.csv` (`code;amount`).
- `chb generate` writes `YYYY/<tier>/annual-accounts.json` (YYYY = end of
  the fiscal period) and `latest/<tier>/annual-accounts.json`: key figures,
  every figure, documents with sha256, and consistency checks (balance,
  sums, the "other appropriations" balancing line, carried-forward vs
  balance sheet, unexplained gross margin, negative liabilities, opening
  balance vs the previous fiscal year).
- Public and members get filed fiscal years only, with the abbreviated
  statements copied to `YYYY/public/annual-accounts/`. Drafts, trial
  balances and internal balance sheets stay in the stewards tier.
- Every year has an `annual-accounts.json` (empty `fiscalYears` when none);
  the archive is covered by `hashes.json`.

Docs: `docs/annual-accounts.md`, `docs/website.md` §11, `docs/audiences.md`.
