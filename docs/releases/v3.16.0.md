# v3.16.0 — expenses, vendors, customers, bookings; transaction leak fixed

**Privacy fix.** Public `transactions.json` carried bank narrations for
KBC lines (payer names, account numbers and BICs written with spaces) in
`metadata.description` and in the `fullDescription` tag; 486 public
transactions over 22 months on prod. Bank narrations (KBC, Wise, CSV,
incoming SEPA memos) no longer reach the public tier; tags are projected
like metadata; members keep the narration with account numbers and BICs
masked. The audience policy now also detects IBANs written with spaces and
removes Belgian national register numbers below stewards.
`members/counterparties.json`, refused by the stricter check, is masked
and written again.

**New files**, per month (`YYYY/MM/<tier>/`) and per year (`YYYY/<tier>/`):

- `expenses.json`: every vendor bill, credit note and expense claim, line
  by line (replaces the month `bills.json` of v3.14);
- `vendors.json`: per vendor, category and totals paid and due;
- `customers.json`: per customer, income types and totals received and due;
- `bookings.json`: room bookings from the room calendars, room-rental
  invoice lines (account 700100), per-room occupancy and revenue.

Public names organisations, and VAT-registered sole traders as vendors.
Private individuals appear by type only, merged per category, without free
text. Payroll lines lose their text, and account names are reduced to
their class. Members see every name; stewards see contact details, bank
accounts, payments, attachments and invoice lists. Partners whose name
carries a legal form ("DelivCo SRL …") count as organisations even when
Odoo says "person".

**Pull:** invoice and bill lines now carry their general-ledger account,
and expense reports (hr.expense) are archived per month. Run
`chb invoices pull --since 2024-01 --force` and
`chb bills pull --since 2024-01 --force` once to backfill the accounts.

Docs: `docs/accounting-data.md`, linked from `docs/website.md` §7.
