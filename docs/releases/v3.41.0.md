# v3.41.0 — `chb members recap`

Each member's recap of their use of the hub (days at the hub, tokens
received and spent, praise, #contributions photos, a call to a shift or
event), monthly, quarterly and/or yearly as they choose. The website
renders it and holds the preferences; chb picks the recipients and sends
it, since only chb knows the members' addresses.

- **Recipients**: active and grace members (latest members.json) with a
  Discord id: `accounts.discord` when it is a Discord id, or the stewards'
  map `settings/members-discord.json`
  (`{"odoo:<partner id>": "<discord id>", "stripe:<customer id>": "<discord id>"}`).
  Members without one are counted and skipped, never guessed from names.
- **Preferences**: `GET /api/notifications/subscribers` on the website
  (default monthly). All three off = opted out, never emailed.
- **Due periods**: on days 1–3 of a month, the previous month; in January,
  April, July and October also the previous quarter; in January also the
  previous year. `--period YYYY-MM|YYYY-Qn|YYYY` picks one by hand.
- **Rendering**: `GET /api/recap?discordId=…&period=…` (Bearer
  RENEW_LINK_SECRET); the footer carries the member's settings link.
- **Sending**: Resend, from hello@ with reply-to hello@. Preview by default;
  `--yes` sends at most 25 per run (`--limit N`), the rest go out on the
  next run. Ledger `latest/providers/members/recaps-sent.json`, keyed
  `discordId:period`, so nobody gets a period twice.
- **Test**: `--send-test <email> --discord <id> [--period P]` sends one
  member's recap to one address, without touching the ledger.
- Addresses are never printed unmasked. Not in the hourly cron yet.
