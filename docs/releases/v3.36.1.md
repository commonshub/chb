# v3.36.1 — the invoice wins when membership is at stake

- When the invoice a payment settles (reconciled in Odoo, or named by its
  structured communication) is membership and a rule categorised the
  payment otherwise — or the reverse — the invoice's category wins
  (`metadata.ruleCategory` keeps the rule's). Customer IBAN rules had put
  membership payments (product 104, €200, no VAT) under rental. Other
  disagreements keep the rule's category.
